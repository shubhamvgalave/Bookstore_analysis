## 📌 Project Overview

The **Online Bookstore SQL Data Analysis** project focuses on managing, organizing, and analyzing bookstore data using **MySQL**.

The project consists of three main datasets:

- 📖 **Books** – Book titles, authors, genres, publication years, prices, and stock availability.
- 👥 **Customers** – Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, cities, and countries.
- 🛒 **Orders** – Order dates, customer details, books purchased, quantities, and total amounts.

The primary goal is to simulate a real-world retail business scenario by using SQL queries to extract meaningful insights from bookstore data and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## ❗ Business Problem

An online bookstore generates large amounts of data through book sales, customer registrations, and order transactions. Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to monitor sales performance, manage inventory, identify popular books, and understand customer purchasing behavior.

📊 The bookstore needs an efficient database solution to answer important business questions, such as:

- 📚 Which books belong to specific genres, and which are the most and least expensive?
- 📦 How much stock is available, and which books have low inventory?
- 👤 Which customers place multiple orders or spend the most money?
- 💰 What is the total revenue generated from book sales?
- 🏆 Which genres, authors, and books contribute most to sales?
- 🌍 Which cities have customers who place high-value orders?
- 📉 How much inventory remains after accounting for ordered quantities?

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 🗄️ **Database Management:** Create a relational database with separate tables for books, customers, and orders.
- 📥 **Data Import:** Import CSV datasets into MySQL for structured analysis.
- 🔍 **Data Exploration:** Retrieve relevant information using filtering, sorting, and distinct-value queries.
- 💵 **Sales Analysis:** Calculate total revenue, sales quantities, and frequently ordered books.
- 👥 **Customer Analysis:** Identify repeat customers, high-spending customers, and customer locations.
- 📦 **Inventory Analysis:** Examine stock availability and estimate remaining inventory after orders.
- 📈 **Business Insights:** Use SQL results to support inventory planning and business decisions.

## 🧠 SQL Concepts Used

- ✅ Database and table creation (`CREATE DATABASE`, `CREATE TABLE`)
- ✅ CSV data import (`LOAD DATA INFILE`)
- ✅ Data filtering (`WHERE`, `BETWEEN`)
- ✅ Sorting and limiting (`ORDER BY`, `LIMIT`)
- ✅ Aggregate functions (`SUM()`, `AVG()`, `COUNT()`)
- ✅ Grouping and filtering groups (`GROUP BY`, `HAVING`)
- ✅ Combining tables (`INNER JOIN`, `LEFT JOIN`)
- ✅ Removing duplicate results (`DISTINCT`)
- ✅ Handling missing values (`COALESCE()`)
- ✅ Primary keys and foreign key relationships
